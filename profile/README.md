# TEDxPurdueU

**Ideas worth spreading at Purdue University.**

TEDxPurdueU is an independently organized TEDx event in West Lafayette, Indiana. We bring together students, researchers, founders, artists, and community leaders to share ideas that spark conversation and inspire change.

## What we do

- **Annual TEDx conference** — Six speakers and 300+ attendees come together for a day of ideas and connection.
- **TEDx salons** — Smaller, free events that keep meaningful conversations going throughout the year.
- **Student Speaker Competition** — An opportunity for Purdue students to earn a place on the TEDxPurdueU stage.
